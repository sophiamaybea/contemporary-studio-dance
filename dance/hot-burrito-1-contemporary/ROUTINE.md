## 4. Motifs
- **Motif A — “Showed you how”**: right arm high 5th → spiral torso left → left leg arabesque on pointe (the reference pose seed).
- **Motif B — “Deep inside”**: breath collapse to crouch → sequential spine roll up → off-axis tilt turn.
- **Motif C — “Toy / old boy”**: parallel chassé with lagging arm, then sudden still on relevé.
- **Motif D — Floor sentence**: lateral shoulder roll → knee hover → push to standing arabesque.

`[0:00 | bar 1 | 8-count 1 | 4/4 @ 156]`
Cue — pedal steel swell.

```

`[0:12 | bar 7 | 8-count 4 | 4/4 @ 156]`
Cue — “You may be sweet and nice”

```

### CHORUS 1 (peak)
`[1:02 | bar 33 | 8-count 17 | 4/4 @ 156]`

```

```

`[1:27 | bar 46 | 8-count 24 | 4/4 @ 156]`

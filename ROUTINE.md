# Contemporary Studio Phrase — Sports Shorts / Headphones Dancer

**Slug:** contemporary-studio-phrase  
**Style:** contemporary-forward (release + spiral + weight)  
**Level:** professional  
**Cast:** solo  
**Duration:** ~0:30 (3 x 8-counts + hold)  
**Space:** contemporary dance studio, sprung wooden floor, barre + mirrors  
**Footwear:** barefoot  
**BPM / Meter:** free-tempo contemporary (approx 70 BPM breathing pulse) | 4/4  
**Music cue:** ambient piano / rain / silence — treat as phrase for class or film score  

## Musical architecture
- Free contemporary pulse (dancer breathes the count)
- 3 full 8-counts + final hold
- Accent on weight drops and spiral releases

## Counts

[0:00 | bar 1 | 8-count 1 | free 4/4 @ ~70]

1-2  shift weight left, spiral torso R, R arm opens to 2nd  
3-4  recover centre, both arms float to 5th, soft plié  
5-6  step R to side, spiral L, L arm sweeps low  
7-8  close L, face DS, inhale arms to high V  

[0:08 | bar 3 | 8-count 2]

1-2  fall into L side, spiral floorward, R leg slides tendu  
3-4  recover through spiral, rise on R, arms circle  
5-& 6  soft turn en dedans, spot mirror  
7-8  land 4th parallel, arms to 2nd, eyes soft  

[0:16 | bar 5 | 8-count 3]

1-2  walk DS two steps, weight heavy, arms release  
3-4  spiral down to low crouch, one hand floor  
5-6  reverse spiral up, roll through spine  
7-8  stand tall, arms high, look into mirror  

[0:24 hold]

hold the line 4 counts, breath only, then release  

## Notes for the body
- Long legs, athletic hourglass — full extension on spirals  
- Headphones stay on; movement never knocks them  
- Sports shorts + crop top: clean lines, no costume catch  
- Mirrors are partners — use them for focus, not vanity
